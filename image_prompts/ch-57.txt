A highly divine, mystical, and awe-inspiring digital painting set in an ancient Indian ashram. In the center, a highly devoted ancient ascetic sage, Bhrushundi, is sitting in deep lotus meditation. Miraculously, due to his intense devotion to Lord Ganesha, his face has transformed to feature a mystical, glowing elephant trunk (shunda), which he is gently holding. Standing before him is the royal King Kashiraja, dressed in opulent ancient Indian royal attire, looking absolutely astonished and moved to tears of joy and reverence, with his hands folded in Pranam. Faint, ethereal golden divine light surrounds the sage, symbolizing pure, one-pointed devotion (Ekanishtha Bhakti). Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
