A highly dramatic and classical mythological digital painting showing two contrasting scenes. In the lower half, on earth, the giant, terrifying demon Tripurasura and his demonic army are aggressively destroying a sacred Vedic fire ritual (Yajna), smashing altars and tearing down sacred trees while ancient Indian sages flee in terror towards the dense forest. In the upper half, amidst mystical clouds, the weakened, starving celestial Devas (Gods) with pale faces are humbly bowing down with folded hands, praying to the glowing, serene, and youthful ascetic sage Sanatkumara, who radiates pure, brilliant spiritual golden light. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed, dramatic lighting representing the battle between chaotic evil and divine wisdom.
