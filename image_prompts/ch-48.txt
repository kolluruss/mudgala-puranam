A highly dramatic and intense mythological digital painting of an ancient celestial battlefield. In the center, the giant, terrifying demon general Tarakasura, glowing with dark, aggressive energy, has just struck a powerful blow with his large glowing sword. Lord Indra (Devendra), the king of gods, is depicted falling backward unconscious, dropping his Vajra (thunderbolt). In the background, chaotic dark magic elements like fierce winds and swirling water engulf the battlefield. The demon army cheers while the celestial Devas are seen scattering and fleeing in absolute terror towards the misty, distant Mount Kailash in the background. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed, dramatic lighting, capturing the exact moment of Indra's defeat.
