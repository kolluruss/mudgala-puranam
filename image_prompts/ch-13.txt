A highly serene, mystical, and divine digital painting depicting the grand Hindu cosmic order. At the center, the glowing Dhruva star (Pole Star) holds the entire cosmos together with subtle, glowing, ethereal threads of wind. Orbiting around it are the magnificent celestial chariots of the Nine Planets (Navagrahas). The Sun God is on a radiant golden chariot pulled by seven horses, accompanied by ancient sages and celestial beings. The Moon is on a silvery chariot pulled by white horses. Other planets like Mars, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, Rahu, and Ketu are seen on their unique chariots pulled by rams, eagles, lions, and pigeons respectively. In the cosmic background, a vast, translucent, and peaceful form of Lord Ganesha is gently blessing the orderly motion of the universe. The atmosphere is highly spiritual, tranquil, and ethereal, promoting profound inner peace, divine harmony, and joy. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
