A highly serene, mystical, and divine cosmic digital painting depicting the ascent through the glowing upper celestial realms of Hindu cosmology. Starting from the lower glowing worlds, ascending through luminous Satyaloka (realm of Brahma), Shaktiloka, Vaikuntha (realm of Vishnu), and Kailasa (realm of Shiva). Above all these, at the absolute highest peak of the cosmos, is the infinitely radiant, formless yet profoundly beautiful 'Svananda Loka', where a magnificent, translucent, and supreme divine form of Lord Ganesha sits in deep eternal Samadhi (meditation). The atmosphere is highly spiritual, deeply tranquil, ethereal, and filled with infinite golden and pure white cosmic rays, symbolizing ultimate liberation and supreme bliss. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
