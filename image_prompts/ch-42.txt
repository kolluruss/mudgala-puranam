A highly dramatic and classical mythological digital painting capturing the iconic 'Trimurti Pariksha' (Testing of the Trinity). In the center of the opulent celestial realm of Vaikuntha, the ancient sage Bhrigu is depicted with a fierce expression, having just kicked the chest of Lord Vishnu. Lord Vishnu, with a supremely calm, compassionate, and divine smile, sits on his celestial serpent bed (Shesha) alongside Goddess Lakshmi, gently holding Sage Bhrigu's foot in his hands, massaging it to check if the sage was hurt. Above this scene, seamlessly blended into the ethereal golden cosmic sky, is a majestic, translucent vision of Lord Ganesha in deep meditation, symbolizing the ultimate cosmic peace and the sage's future redemption. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed, vibrant colors, emphasizing divine forgiveness over human pride.
