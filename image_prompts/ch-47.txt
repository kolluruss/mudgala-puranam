A highly dramatic and epic mythological digital painting of a fierce ancient battle in Shaktiloka. In the foreground, Lord Indra (Devendra), glowing with divine fury, sits atop his majestic multi-trunked white elephant Airavata, wielding his glowing Vajra (thunderbolt). Opposite him stands the giant, heavily armored demon general Tarakasura, laughing arrogantly and brandishing dark weapons. The background is a chaotic, fiery battlefield filled with fighting celestial forces (Devas and Shaktis) and demonic entities (Asuras), with rivers of blood on the ground. Above the clouds, subtle divine feminine energies (Goddess forms) oversee the destruction. The atmosphere is tense, aggressive, and highly cinematic. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
