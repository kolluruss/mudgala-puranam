A highly dramatic and epic mythological digital painting. In a grand, dark, and opulent demonic court, Lord Vishnu, radiating a calm, pure blue divine light and holding his Conch and Discus, stands as a peaceful messenger. He is firmly but serenely addressing the giant, heavily armored, and arrogant demon king Durmati, who sits on a dark throne, pointing a mocking finger back at Vishnu. The contrast between the divine serenity of Vishnu and the aggressive darkness of the demon king is prominent. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed, dramatic cinematic lighting.
