A highly epic, chaotic, and dramatic mythological digital painting of a catastrophic celestial battlefield. In the center, the terrifying demon king Madasura and his fierce sons (Vilasi, Lolupa, Dhanapriya) are overpowering the supreme Hindu Gods. Lord Vishnu is depicted falling backward from his majestic eagle mount, Garuda, dropping his glowing Sudarshana Chakra after being struck by a mace. Nearby, Lord Shiva and Surya (Sun God) are shown being entangled in dark, glowing magical nooses by demonic forces. The demon army roars in triumphant victory with dark storm clouds gathering above. In the background, the remaining celestial Devas scatter in absolute terror, looking up towards the heavens to pray to a faint, protective vision of Goddess Jagadamba. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, dark and aggressive lighting, highly detailed.
