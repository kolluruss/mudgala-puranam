Planets, moons, and constellations gracefully orbit in perfect harmony. The atmosphere is highly spiritual, tranquil, and ethereal, filled with soft golden cosmic rays. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed, promoting profound inner peace, universal harmony, and spiritual joy.
