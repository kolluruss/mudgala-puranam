A highly dramatic and divine mythological digital painting set in an ancient Indian forest. In the foreground, a mystical scene shows the demon Madasura, whose body is reduced to a skeletal form covered in an ancient anthill and creepers, performing intense penance. Descending from the glowing heavens is the magnificent, multi-armed Supreme Goddess Adishakti (Mahamaya), radiantly glowing and majestically seated on a fierce lion. She is showering mystical, healing divine water upon him, which is transforming his skeletal form back into a powerful, muscular demon king. The atmosphere is highly spiritual, filled with cosmic golden light and ancient mystical energy. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
