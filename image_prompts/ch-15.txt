A highly serene, mystical, and breathtaking cosmic digital painting depicting the ancient Hindu cosmology of the subterranean worlds (Patalas). The scene transitions downwards: showcasing the beautiful, glowing, jewel-lit Patala Lokas with majestic celestial palaces, lush trees, and Naga deities. Deep below them, the massive, magnificent 1000-headed cosmic serpent, Adishesha, floats in the cosmic ocean, emanating a divine, soft golden aura. Beneath Adishesha is the colossal divine Tortoise (Kurma Avatar), and further down is the fierce, gigantic divine Boar (Varaha Avatar) holding the cosmos. Above it all, a transcendent, ethereal, and translucent form of Lord Ganesha peacefully blesses the entire universal structure. The atmosphere is deeply spiritual, majestic, and awe-inspiring, filled with cosmic rays and vibrant celestial colors. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
