A highly divine, serene, and deeply philosophical mythological digital painting set in an ancient Indian ashram. In the center, the youthful, glowing, and supremely wise sage Kapila Muni is seated, gently raising his hand in a gesture of teaching (Chin Mudra). He is passionately explaining the cosmic secrets of the five elements and universal creation to his attentive parents, Sage Kardama and the noble mother Devahuti, who are seated before him with folded hands and expressions of deep spiritual awakening. Faint, ethereal glowing symbols of the five elements (Earth, Water, Fire, Air, Space) and the single-syllable Om/Gam mantra float gently in the mystical air around them. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, natural lighting, highly detailed, capturing a moment of supreme Vedantic enlightenment.
