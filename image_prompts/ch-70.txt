A dramatic and highly tense mythological digital painting set in a peaceful, ancient Indian forest ashram. The giant, heavily armored, and arrogant demon king Ganasura is aggressively forcefully snatching a brilliantly glowing, magical divine jewel (the Chintamani) from the hands of the serene and wise ascetic sage, Kapila Muni. Kapila Muni looks on with a mix of shock and rising righteous anger. In the background, leftovers of a lavish, magical divine feast are visible, and the demon army stands watching. The atmosphere contrasts the calm, holy aura of the ashram with the dark, greedy energy of the demon. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, dramatic cinematic lighting, highly detailed.
