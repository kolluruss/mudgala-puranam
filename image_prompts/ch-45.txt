A highly dramatic and epic mythological digital painting. The terrifying and invincible demon king Madasura, glowing with dark power, proudly sits on the divine, glowing throne of Lord Vishnu (Narayana) in the celestial realm of Vaikuntha, surrounded by his fierce demon generals enjoying heavenly pleasures. In the ethereal background, celestial gardens and Kalpavriksha trees are seen overtaken by demons. High above in the mystical cosmic clouds, the defeated Devas (Gods) including Indra and Brahma are seen humbly taking refuge at the lotus feet of the Supreme Goddess Adishakti, who sits majestically on a fierce lion, radiating a protective, golden divine aura. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
