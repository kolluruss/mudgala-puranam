A highly dramatic and divine mythological digital painting set in the ethereal, mystical celestial realm of Goloka. The supremely beautiful Goddess Radha, glowing with divine feminine fury and wearing rich traditional attire, is angrily pointing a finger, casting a powerful magical curse upon Lord Krishna's devoted celestial friend Sridama. Sridama, looking deeply angered and defiant, is casting a curse back at her. In the background, the glowing, clear waters of the newly formed mystical Viraja river flow through lush celestial gardens. The atmosphere is tense, filled with glowing magical aura, ethereal mist, and dramatic emotional lighting. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
