A deeply serene, highly mystical, and spiritual digital painting set in an ancient Indian forest. The ascetic sage Jadabharata, dressed in simple rags but radiating a brilliant, profound golden aura of supreme cosmic wisdom, is calmly seated teaching. Before him, King Rahugana, having abandoned his royal palanquin and rich jewelry in the background, sits on the forest floor with folded hands in complete surrender and deep reverence. Above them in the soft forest canopy, a translucent, ethereal, and divine form of Lord Ganesha gracefully manifests, showering golden cosmic rays that symbolize 'Svananda' (Supreme Bliss) and the dissolution of worldly illusions. The atmosphere is tranquil, deeply meditative, and purely divine. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
