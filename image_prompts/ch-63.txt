A highly divine, mystical, and serene digital painting set in a dense, ancient Indian forest. Lord Shiva, dressed as an ascetic in tiger skin, and Goddess Parvati are sitting devotedly on the banks of the holy river Ganges. They are reverently performing pooja (worship) to a beautiful, hand-crafted earthen (clay) idol of Lord Ganesha, offering fresh green Durva grass. Hovering faintly in the glowing sky above is the majestic, transcendent, and blessing form of Lord Ekadanta Ganesha, radiating pure golden celestial light, granting them the power to defeat the arrogant planetary deity Shani. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, detailed nature, dramatic and spiritual lighting.
