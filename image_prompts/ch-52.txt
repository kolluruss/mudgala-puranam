A highly divine, serene, and mystical digital painting set in a lush, peaceful ancient Indian forest. The celestial Devas (including Indra, Brahma, Vishnu) and ancient ascetic sages are awakening from deep, century-long meditation, looking amazed and overjoyed. Before them manifests the glorious, transcendent form of Lord Ekadanta Ganesha, radiating pure spiritual golden light. Ganesha is majestic and calming, seated gracefully on his giant, divine mouse mount (Mooshika). The Devas and sages are kneeling and bowing down with folded hands in pure devotion. The atmosphere is filled with cosmic peace, divine blessings, and glowing ethereal light. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, highly detailed.
