A highly epic, cinematic, and divine mythological digital painting of a fierce cosmic battle in the ancient holy city of Kashi. In the center, Lord Ganesha in his colossal, terrifying 'Gajanana' (elephant-faced) Virat Roopa, radiating intense divine power and golden light. He is fiercely engaged in combat with the giant, dark, heavily armored demon king Devantaka. Devantaka has just managed to break Ganesha's left tusk. In swift divine retaliation, the supreme Lord Ganesha is dynamically wielding his own freshly broken, glowing left tusk as a mighty weapon, striking down the demon Devantaka. The background features the ancient temples and ghats of Kashi beneath a turbulent, stormy celestial sky. Rich traditional Hindu aesthetic, dramatic action lighting, highly detailed, capturing the exact moment of the demon's defeat by the broken tusk.
